Maps & Projections

 

Region Map Explorer

Sibling of the City Map Explorer at regional scale — pick a well-known geographic region and render its extent at exactly the chosen pixel size and radius, clipped to a circle. Toggle Railways for an OpenRailwayMap overlay. Use Save PNG to export a lossless image of the visible circle.

Great Circle Mapper

A world map you can re-project live, entirely in the browser. Pick a coordinate framing (Eastern / Western / Northern / Southern) and a projection (Winkel Tripel or Hǎo's 等差分多圆锥 vertical map), toggle country borders, and draw great-circle flight paths — the preset routes, or your own: type 3-letter IATA codes such as PEK-JFK, LHR-HND-SIN. It runs on the same projection maths as my printed-map reconstruction. Coastlines & borders: Natural Earth (public domain); airports: OurAirports (public domain).
