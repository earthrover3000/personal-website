Cities & Transport

 

City Map Explorer

Pick a city and edge length to render its map at exactly that pixel size, clipped to a circle. Toggle Railways to overlay OpenRailwayMap — useful for comparing the railway/metro footprint of cities at a common 17 km radius. Use Save PNG to export a lossless image of the visible circle.

Cities

  • LuminoCity3D — interactive 3D atlas of urban density, height, and sprawl across world cities.
  • GaWC: The World According to GaWC — the Globalization and World Cities ranking from Loughborough University; source for this page's city list.

Transport

  • Cartometro — hand-drawn schematic maps of the world's metro, tram, and suburban-rail networks at a common visual scale.
