Food intolerances👤🪪⚗️💊

Foreword

I don’t have philosophical food restrictions. However, I have a large number of neurodivergence-related food intolerances. Please find below my food intolerances classified in pharmacological/chemical class (for better understanding) and food type class (for logistics of cooking & eating out). All chemical active ingredients are hypothesised, subject to reclassification and modification in the future.
